The Human

(02) About

I started out drawing posters for a friend’s band. Fifteen years later the tools changed, the itch never did.

I work at the seam between design and code — which means I don’t hand off a picture and hope. I build the thing, I feel the timing of it, and I fix what feels wrong at 2am.

Most of my work sits with studios, founders and cultural outfits who’d rather be remembered than liked. I take on a small number of projects a year so each one gets the attention it deserves — no conveyor belt, no template thinking.

Arjun Mehra Designer & Developer — Mumbai / Remote
